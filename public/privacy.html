Privacy

Effective October 21, 2026

Cairn is a small, free to-do app made by Rodion and shared with friends. It shows no ads and sells nothing.

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing leaves your device. Your tasks live in your browser's storage.
  • With an account, your tasks are end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase only you know before upload. We can't read them, and neither can our hosting provider.
  • We keep the minimum: your email (for sign-in codes) and the encrypted backup.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising cookies, no selling or sharing of data.
  • You can delete everything at any time from the app.
  • The assistant is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, the text of a new task plus the titles, areas, due dates and priorities of your open tasks go to Anthropic's Claude API to be sorted. Notes and steps are never sent.

We never receive your passphrase, your encryption key, or the contents of your tasks.

How your data is protected

  • On your device: stored in IndexedDB. The encryption key is stored as a non-extractable key that the page can use but not read.
  • In transit: HTTPS only.
  • On the server: AES-GCM-256 encryption done in your browser, with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations, random salt). Database rules let each account read and write only its own row.

If you forget your passphrase, we cannot recover your backup. Your tasks remain on any device that still has them, and you can create a new backup from there.

The optional assistant

Settings → Assistant: Off (default, nothing is sent), My own API key (stored only in this browser and sent only to api.anthropic.com, billed to you), or Invite code if offered (requests go through the operator's Cloudflare Worker, which checks the code, rate-limits it, and is written never to store or log task text). Notes, steps, plans, finished tasks and your sync passphrase are never sent.

Where data is stored

The encrypted backup and your email are stored with Supabase. The app itself is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses like any web host.

How long we keep it

  • Encrypted backups and your email: until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account (Sync across devices → Delete account) removes your email and backup immediately. Provider backups age out on their own schedule.
  • Local data: until you clear it in Settings or clear your browser's site data.

Your choices

Use Cairn without an account; export your data any time (Settings → Back up); delete your account yourself; or ask questions by opening an issue at github.com/rmaytr/TasksApp or contacting Rodion directly.

Children

Cairn is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Because your tasks are encrypted with your passphrase, no future change can give us access to past backups.
